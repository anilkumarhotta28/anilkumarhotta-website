About

Numbers by training, curiosity by nature.

I'm Anil Kumar Hotta, a CA Intermediate student from Brahmapur, Odisha, pursuing Chartered Accountancy under the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India. I also hold a B.Com (Hons) from Khallikote Unitary University, completed with a CGPA of 8.63 after switching from science to commerce. Mathematics has been a constant thread — from the PMSTS scholarship at school to the analytical discipline I now bring to accounting, taxation and audit.

Alongside my studies, I work on real assignments. Since September 2024 I have freelanced for Ambitious Professionals, a coaching institute for CA and CMA students — handling digital marketing, graphic design, YouTube and Shorts editing, and building their website. An internship at Welcome Associates, an advocate and tax consultancy firm, gave me hands-on experience in ITR filing, tax audits under Forms 3CB-3CD, GST reconciliation and Tally. I have also completed job simulations with PwC, EY, KPMG, Deloitte, Bank of America and Fidelity.

Beyond work, I follow finance, artificial intelligence and geopolitics closely. I am also a touch typist — at 80+ words per minute, I am around the top 5% of typists worldwide. I believe the process is always in our hands; the result never fully is.
