Projects

Financial Performance Analysis of Selected Income Taxpayers (Businessmen) of Brahmapur City

My B.Com (Hons) final-year research project at Khallikote Unitary University, guided by Dr. Annapurna Sahoo, Head of the Department of Commerce.

The study examines the financial performance and tax compliance behaviour of seven businesses across Brahmapur — spanning retail, manufacturing, FMCG trading, and services — over three financial years (2022–23 to 2024–25). Using ratio analysis, trend analysis, cash flow analysis, and correlation analysis on real profit & loss accounts and balance sheets, it evaluates profitability, liquidity, solvency, and how business structure shapes tax reporting.

A key finding: sales and net profit show an exceptionally strong positive correlation (above 0.99) across all three years, while service and retail businesses consistently posted higher margins than volume-driven manufacturing and trading units.
